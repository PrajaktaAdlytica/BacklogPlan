## Intentional adaptation of the visual mock

The selected mock is visual direction, not evidence of a product. Its inconsistent names, dates, manual procedure text, review timing and planning window were replaced by the internally consistent synthetic fixture. The decorative timeline above the pump was omitted; the actual revision controls and schedule are interactive HTML. Additional pages and content chapters implement the full brief beyond the short visual mock.

## Before a public launch

A public launch is not part of this local prototype handoff. It needs authorized hosting/domain configuration, verified company/contact details and brand clearance, approved legal policies, an accessible feedback channel, a consented lead-delivery service, retention/deletion handling and a reviewed analytics setup. Production authentication/integrations are a separate product scope. A real pilot additionally needs the named planner/sponsor/operator/data owner, procurement/legal/security permission, historical comparator, sandbox responsibility and paid SOW. Brand source imagery is raster; a professionally finalized vector identity can follow selection approval.

No Git commit, push, deployment, real-data import, external message, ad campaign or payment action was performed.
